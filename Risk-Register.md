Risk Register – Customer Churn Prediction

1. Small Dataset

Risk: The dataset contains only 12 records, which is not enough to train a reliable model.

Solution: Collect more customer data before using the model in real-world applications.

2. Prediction Errors

Risk: The model may incorrectly predict whether a customer will leave.

Solution: Evaluate the model using accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.

3. Data Privacy

Risk: Customer information may be exposed or misused.

Solution: Protect customer data and restrict unauthorized access.

4. Bias in Predictions

Risk: The model may produce unfair predictions for certain customer groups.

Solution: Check the dataset for bias and review predictions regularly.

5. Data Quality

Risk: Incorrect, duplicate or missing data may affect predictions.

Solution: Clean and verify the dataset before training.

6. Human Supervision

Risk: Businesses may make decisions based only on model predictions.

Solution: Keep human review before taking important customer-related decisions.

Conclusion

Identifying and managing risks helps build safer, fairer and more reliable machine learning systems.
